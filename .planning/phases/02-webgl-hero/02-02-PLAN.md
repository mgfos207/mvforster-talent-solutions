| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-04 | Tampering | pointer coords -> stepNodes | accept | Coordinates are only used as numbers in distance math; never rendered as text/HTML, never persisted or sent anywhere; buffer builder is unit-tested to emit finite, clamped values |
